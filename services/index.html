Our Services

Comprehensive acoustic engineering and professional audio solutions, engineered for your space.

From full acoustic soundproofing to sound and lighting rentals, Gib Rabboni Limited delivers every layer of acoustic and audio infrastructure your project needs — under one engineering-led team, from first survey to final handover.

Acoustic Soundproofing

We isolate sound at its source, preventing noise transfer between rooms and to the outside world. Our soundproofing solutions are engineered using mass, decoupling, and sealing principles — not just added material — to deliver measurable noise reduction.

Key Benefits

  • Significant reduction in airborne and impact noise transfer
  • Improved privacy and comfort for occupants
  • Compliance support for noise-sensitive environments

Ideal Applications

Recording studios, home theatres, offices, residential units, and commercial partitions.

Industries Served

Hospitality, corporate, residential, entertainment, education.

Acoustic Treatments

Acoustic treatment shapes how sound behaves inside a room — controlling reverberation, reflections, and standing waves so every space sounds balanced and intentional, not harsh or hollow.

Key Benefits

  • Reduced reverberation time for clearer speech and music
  • Elimination of flutter echo and problem reflections
  • Balanced frequency response across the room

Ideal Applications

Studios, sanctuaries, conference rooms, auditoriums, and listening rooms.

Industries Served

Religious institutions, media, corporate, education, entertainment.

Live Music Studio Soundproofing

Live instrumentation demands isolation that regular soundproofing can't provide. We design room-within-a-room solutions that contain high sound-pressure levels while preserving natural acoustics inside the tracking room.

Key Benefits

  • High-level isolation for drums, amps, and live ensembles
  • Preserved natural room tone for authentic recordings
  • Reduced spill between live and control rooms

Ideal Applications

Live tracking rooms, rehearsal studios, band practice rooms.

Industries Served

Music production, entertainment, education.
