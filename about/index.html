About Gib Rabboni Limited

Engineering excellence behind every sound we shape.

Our Story

Built on a Passion for Sound Done Right

Gib Rabboni Limited was founded on a simple observation: too many spaces in Nigeria were being built beautifully, then let down by acoustics that were never designed, only assumed. We set out to change that — bringing rigorous acoustic engineering to churches, studios, hotels, and entertainment venues that deserved better than off-the-shelf foam and guesswork.

Over the years, that founding idea has grown into a full-service acoustic engineering and professional audio company, trusted by clients across Lagos and beyond to deliver installations that are engineered, tested, and built to last.

What Drives Us

Mission, Vision & Core Values
